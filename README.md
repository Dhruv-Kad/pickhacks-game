## Introduction
In this game, the world is on the brink of chaos, and the power to shape the future rests in your hands.  
Your mission is simple: stop the spread of the Bloviet Munion's influence at any cost.
##  
In this game, you will use a tactical map to decide your next move. Simply select an action and input coordinates to carry out your commands.

1. **Placing a Factory**:  
   Building factories will boost your resources. For each factory you place, you will earn an additional $250 per round.  
   Factories must be placed within a ten-unit radius of your base. You will also need to watch where your opponent places their factories to get an idea of where their base is located.

2. **Starting a Coup**:  
   Fund your 'peaceful rebels' to set up a base with nuclear capabilities. However, be warned: if the enemy is nearby, your faction's base might get pulled into a conflict.  
   You can invest more money to reduce the chance of enemy interference, but this comes with a cost.

3. **Launching a Nuke**:  
  Will instantly raise the Munion to high alert, and signal the start of nuclear war.
4. **Hiring Spies**:  
   Your spy network can help you gather intel on enemy positions. The more money you invest in spies, the better they will reveal enemy bases.  
   But remember, the Munion isn't without its own spies, and they are deadly efficient.

5. **Passing Your Turn**:  
   You also have the option to pass your turn if you find yourself low on funds.

## Tension 
The Munion won't start a nuclear conflict without a valid reason. Your actions will increase the **Tension Bar** at the bottom of the screen, indicating how close the game is to reaching a nuclear showdown.  
Every action you take (except building factories) will raise the tension by one point. If you go nuclear, be aware that the Munion will retaliate with its own nuclear strikes.
## To win 
  To win, destroy all enemy bunkers.

# Additional information

**Controls** \
Keyboard only, follow prompts onscreen and submit with [ENTER]

**Difficulty** \
There is only one difficulty. Non-Proliferation is a harsh game.

**Game Start** \
At the beginning of the game, place one starting base down

## Shop
**Nuke** \
Destroys everything in a 2 unit radius centered on the target tile \
Instantly sets tension to 9

**Spy** \
Gives a small chance to reveal an enemy base per turn, increases with number of spies

**Factory** \
Increases income per factory

**Coup** \
Select a tile. If there are no enemy bases within a 3/2/1 tile radius, create a base \
If there is an enemy base in said area, there is a 50/70/90% chance of destroying it \
Increases tension by 1
